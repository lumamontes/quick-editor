    Pick a photo on the left.

    Put photos in the inbox folder shown on the left. JPG, PNG, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF and WebP work.

    Frame the page

    Drag the four corners onto the page's corners. Everything outside is ignored and the page is straightened.

    Clean

    Areas

    Any shape, any number of points. Click to place points; click the first point (or double-click, or Enter) to close. Esc cancels, Backspace undoes a point. Click an area to select it, Delete removes it.

    Pieces

    Box: drag on empty space to draw one. Lasso: click points around a drawing, any shape; click the first point to close. Click a piece to select it (shift-click for more), drag to move, drag a box's corners to resize. Delete removes.

    Review & save

    Saved pieces go into the project's catalog as drafts ("status": "draft").
